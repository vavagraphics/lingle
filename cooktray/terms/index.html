6.2 The AI Recipe Finder

When you ask the AI Recipe Finder for a recipe, CookTray does the following on your behalf, and only when you ask:

  • searches the public web for highly rated recipes that match your request;
  • skips websites that tell automated tools not to access them, including through their robots.txt files, and websites we have chosen to exclude;
  • reads only the factual recipe information that the website publishes for search engines, such as ingredients, quantities, times and ratings;
  • picks one recipe and writes new, plain step-by-step instructions in its own words, keeping the same ingredients, quantities and method;
  • checks that the new text does not copy the original wording, and does not copy any photos, stories or other creative content;
  • shows the original site's rating as a rating "on the original site", with a "View original" link to the source page;
  • discards the source page once your result is ready. We do not store copies of the websites it reads.

Recipe titles in the AI Recipe Finder are written by CookTray. Ratings belong to the original websites and their users. CookTray is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any website it reads, and we do not show website names or logos in a way that suggests otherwise.
