CookTrayRecipeFinder

CookTrayRecipeFinder is the web reader behind the AI Recipe Finder in the CookTray app by VAVA Graphics, Charlotte, North Carolina.

  • It runs only when a CookTray user asks for a recipe. It does not crawl, and it does not build an index.
  • It identifies itself as CookTrayRecipeFinder/1.0 and obeys robots.txt. Sites that disallow it, or that ban AI agents, are skipped.
  • It never works around blocks, logins, paywalls or bot checks.
  • It reads only the schema.org Recipe data a page publishes for search engines, then discards the page. It does not copy text, photos or stories.
  • Results link back to the original page.

To exclude your site, add this to your robots.txt, or email tristan@vavagraphics.com and we will add it to our exclusion list, usually within one business day:

User-agent: CookTrayRecipeFinder
Disallow: /
