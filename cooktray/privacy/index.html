CookTray Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026 (version 1.1)

This Privacy Policy explains how VAVA Graphics, Charlotte, North Carolina ("VAVA Graphics", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the CookTray app for Android ("CookTray"). It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

1. The short version

  • CookTray has no accounts, no ads and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Your recipes, lists, pantry items and settings are stored on your device.
  • Photo scanning for recipes happens on your device. A photo is uploaded only if you choose AI card reading for it, and it is deleted as soon as it has been read.
  • Only when you use an online feature (the AI Recipe Finder, the AI pantry photo, AI card reading or "Report this") does CookTray send the specific content that feature needs to our servers. We use it to give you the result and then discard it.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

2. Information that stays on your device

The following information is created and stored only on your device. We cannot see it:

  • recipes you type, speak, import from links or scan from screenshots and photos;
  • shopping lists, pantry items, use-by dates, notes and settings;
  • the photos and screenshots CookTray reads to find recipes;
  • your search history in the app.

Photo scanning. CookTray uses on-device text recognition to read the screenshots and photos you pick with Android's photo picker, so it can see only the photos you select each time. It does not ask for access to your whole photo library. If a later version offers that as an option, it will ask you first and you can turn it off at any time in Android settings. Scanned photos are not uploaded to us or anyone else, unless you choose AI card reading for a card or page (see section 3).

Device backups. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include CookTray's data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us. You can also export your recipes to a file you control.

3. Information sent when you use online features

These features need our servers. CookTray sends only what each feature needs, and only when you use it.

Link import. When you share a recipe link with CookTray, your phone opens that one web page directly, the same way a browser does, and reads the recipe on your device. It does not go through our servers.

We also process a small amount of technical information so the online features work and cannot be abused:

  • A random installation ID created by CookTray when you first open it. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Basic service records, such as the time of a request, which feature was used, whether it worked and how much computing it used. These records do not contain your request text, photos or recipe content.

Voice. Speak-to-recipe uses your device's speech recognition. CookTray does not record or upload your audio. Where on-device recognition is not available, Android's own speech service may process the audio under Google's terms.

4. Service providers

We use a small number of service providers to run the online features. They process information only on our instructions and only to provide their service to us:

  • cloud hosting, which runs our servers in the United States;
  • AI model providers, which create recipe text and read pantry photos and recipe cards. We use only paid service tiers whose terms say our requests are not used to train their models;
  • a web search provider, which receives search terms based on your AI Recipe Finder request, but not your installation ID or purchase information;
  • Google, which provides Google Play billing and Android system services under Google's own privacy policy.

5. How long we keep information

  • Request content (requests, photos, text sent for AI features): kept in memory only for the time it takes to return your result, then discarded.
  • Service records: deleted after 30 days.
  • Usage counts for the AI feature limits, stored under a coded version of your installation ID: kept while you use the AI features and for up to 90 days after your last use.
  • Reports you send with "Report this": kept for up to 12 months so we can review them and improve safety, then deleted.
  • Emails you send us: kept as long as needed to answer you and for our normal business records.

Our AI and hosting providers may keep limited records for their own security and abuse monitoring for the short periods set out in their terms with us.

6. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • provide the features you ask for;
  • confirm purchases and apply usage limits;
  • protect CookTray and its users against fraud and abuse;
  • review reports and improve safety;
  • answer your questions and support requests;
  • meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or build advertising profiles.

7. Deleting your information

CookTray has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. You can delete your information in these ways:

  • On your device: delete recipes, lists or pantry items in the app, or uninstall CookTray to remove all of its data from your device.
  • On our servers: tap "Delete my server data" in CookTray's Settings. This deletes the usage counts and reports linked to your installation. You can also email us at tristan@vavagraphics.com and we will delete it for you.
  • Purchases: your purchase history is kept by Google Play in your Google account. Manage it through Google.

8. Children

CookTray is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, please email us and we will delete it.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy of it, to correct it, to delete it, and to opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising. Because CookTray stores your content on your device and keeps very little on our servers, most of these requests can be met directly in the app. To make any request, email us at tristan@vavagraphics.com. We will not treat you differently for using your privacy rights. We may need to confirm the request relates to your installation before acting on it.

10. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all information sent between CookTray and our servers, keep our provider keys on our servers rather than in the app, and limit the information we keep. No system is perfectly secure, but we design CookTray so that most of your information never leaves your device.

11. Where information is processed

Our servers and service providers are located in the United States. If you use CookTray from another country, information sent for online features is processed in the United States.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make a significant change, we will let you know in the app or on the CookTray listing before it takes effect. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
