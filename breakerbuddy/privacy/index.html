Breaker Buddy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026. App by VAVA Apps (VAVA Graphics), Charlotte, North Carolina.

The short version

Breaker Buddy collects no personal data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What the app stores

Your homes, panels, breakers, rooms, devices and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The app keeps daily backup copies in that same private storage.

Backups you control

  • If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; VAVA Apps cannot see it.
  • When you choose to save a backup file, print, or share a PDF or spreadsheet, it goes only where you send it (for example Drive, email or a printer).

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment; the app only receives confirmation that you own Pro. VAVA Apps does not receive your payment details.

Network use

The app does not send your data to any server. It only talks to Google Play to check prices and purchases.

Children

Breaker Buddy is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: tristan@vavagraphics.com.
