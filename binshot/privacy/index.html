Privacy Policy for PaperShot

Effective date: October 19, 2026

PaperShot (listed on Google Play as "PaperShot: Paper Toss Reloaded") is a paper toss game for Android made by VAVA Graphics LLC, Charlotte, North Carolina, USA.

The short version

  • Playing alone, playing pass and play on one phone, and sharing challenge links send nothing to us.
  • If you play a live room with a friend, your display name, your chosen character, your throws and a random ID for your copy of the game go to our room server while the room is open. When the room closes, it is all gone. We keep nothing.
  • Every player is on the public leaderboard: your leaderboard name (your 2-player name or a generated one like "Swift Falcon 482", which you can change), your character, your scores and a random ID for your copy of the game are stored on our server and shown on the public boards until you remove yourself in Settings.
  • The game checks our server for news, offers and update notices. That check sends nothing about you: no ID, no name, no scores.
  • No accounts. No sign-in. We never ask for your real name, email address or phone number.
  • Anonymous gameplay stats (how throws, levels and modes go) help us tune the game. They carry a random ID made only for this, never your name, and you can turn them off in Settings. No advertising, no tracking across apps, no ad ID and no crash reporting.
  • We do not sell or share personal data. Our one server runs live rooms (which save nothing), the anonymous gameplay stats described below, the leaderboard (which saves only what is described below) and the game's news and offers (which receive nothing about you).
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

What the game keeps, and where

Everything the game keeps stays on your phone, in the game's private storage:

  • your progress: levels, stars, bosses cleared, laps and best scores
  • in-game cash, upgrades, boosts and ball skins (in-game cash cannot be bought with real money)
  • Daily Shot results and your Daily Shot streak
  • your settings, such as sound and the daily reminder
  • whether the full game and the Supporter Pack are unlocked on this phone, so they work offline
  • the display name you choose for 2-player games, and a random ID the game makes for itself so you can rejoin a live room

None of this is sent to us, except as described under Live rooms and Leaderboard below. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it from the phone.

Android backup

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android includes the game's save in your phone's normal backup to your own Google account, so a new phone can bring your progress back. Android does this, not the game, and we cannot see these backups. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Save codes

You can copy your progress as a save code and enter it on another phone. The code only goes where you choose to put it. The game never sends it anywhere.

Live rooms

Live rooms let you play a friend on another phone using a room code. Live rooms are optional. Single-player games and pass and play work without them.

When you create or join a live room, the game connects to our room server over an encrypted connection and sends:

  • the short display name you typed, so your friend knows who they are playing
  • the character you picked
  • your throws, scores and power-ups during the match, so your friend's phone can show them
  • a random ID made by the game on your phone, so you can get back into the room if your connection drops

Your display name, character and throws are shown to the other player in your room. The server holds this only in memory while the room is open. It does not write it to a database or a file, and it does not log names or throws. A room closes when the players leave, when the player who opened it leaves, or after 15 minutes with no activity, and everything about it is deleted at that moment. There are no accounts.

The server is hosted by Railway. Like any website, the connection uses your internet (IP) address. The room server uses it only while you are connected, and for up to two minutes after, to limit abuse such as too many rooms opened at once. It does not store it.

The random ID stays on your phone (and in your phone's backup, if backup is on) until you clear the game's data or uninstall it. It is not tied to your Google account or to any other app.

Leaderboard

PaperShot has public leaderboards for the furthest level reached, total stars and the Daily Shot. Every player is on them from the first launch, under their 2-player display name or, if they haven't set one, a generated name like "Swift Falcon 482". The game asks once if you want to keep that name or pick another, and you can change it any time in Settings.

The game sends to our server, over an encrypted connection:

  • your leaderboard name (we block offensive names)
  • the character you play as, so your row shows their face
  • your furthest level, which area of the map it is in, and your total stars
  • your best Daily Shot score for each day you play it, with that day's date
  • the random ID the game made for your copy of the game (the same one live rooms use), so your scores stay on one row

The game sends your latest scores when it starts, after each level and after each Daily Shot. Your name, character and scores are shown to everyone who opens the leaderboard. The server keeps them in a database hosted by Railway until you remove them. Like any website, the connection uses your internet (IP) address; the server uses it only to limit abuse, such as too many requests at once, and does not store it.

To remove yourself, open Settings and tap "Remove me from the leaderboard". That deletes your name, your scores and your Daily Shot results from our server straight away. Your progress on your phone stays. Settings can put you back on the boards. You can also email us to ask for removal.

News, offers and update notices

When the game starts, and when you come back to it (at most every 15 minutes), it asks our server for a short list of messages: a notice when a newer version of the game is out, announcements, and limited-time offers. The request is a plain download. It carries no ID, no name, no scores and no information about your phone or your game. The game itself decides which messages to show you, for example by comparing its own version number and today's date with the message, and remembers on your phone which ones it has already shown. A message's picture is downloaded from the web address in the message and kept on your phone.

Like any website, the connection uses your internet (IP) address. The server uses it only to limit abuse, such as too many requests at once, and does not store it. The last list is kept on your phone so the game works offline.

Updates and reviews through Google Play

When a newer version is out, the game can ask Google Play to download it in the background while you play, then restart to finish. After a good moment in the game, it may ask Google Play to show its rating card. Both are handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. Google Play decides when its rating card appears, and we are not told whether you rated or reviewed the game.

Anonymous gameplay stats

To see which levels are too hard, how the wind feels and which modes people enjoy, the game sends us anonymous gameplay stats. This is on unless you turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous gameplay stats"). The stats are:

  • each throw: the swipe's speed and angle, how far the bin was, the wind, the assist, whether it went in and where it landed, and the mode, level, room and difficulty
  • what happens in the game: opening the app, starting and finishing a round or a level (makes and stars), the mode you pick, power-ups earned and used, opening the full game offer screen, bonus rounds, Arcade visits, and 2-player matches starting and ending
  • the game's version, whether the full game is unlocked, and your phone's Android version number
  • a random ID the game makes for these stats only. It is not your leaderboard or live room ID, and it is never sent with your name.
  • a scrambled device marker, so that reinstalling the game is not counted as a new player. It is made from the ID Android gives PaperShot on your phone (an ID that is different for every app, so it cannot be matched with other apps), run through a one-way scramble (a hash) so the original ID is never sent.

The stats do not include your name, email address, account, contacts, location, advertising ID or any ID of your phone. The game saves them on your phone and sends them a few at a time, over an encrypted connection, to our server, hosted by Railway, where they are kept for up to 400 days and used only to improve the game. We do not sell them or share them with anyone. Like any website, the connection uses your internet (IP) address; the server uses it only to limit abuse and does not store it.

Turning the stats off stops all sending at once, deletes any that are waiting on your phone, and deletes from our server everything sent under that random ID, together with its device marker. If you turn them on again, a new random ID is used.

Challenge links

You can challenge a friend by sharing a link or a code from the game. The challenge, including your display name, score and throws, is packed into the link itself, after the # sign, so it is never sent to our web page or server. It only goes to the people you choose to send it to, through the app you pick to share it.

Daily reminder

The daily reminder is off unless you turn it on. If you do, newer versions of Android ask for permission to show notifications, and the game schedules one notification a day on your phone at the time you pick. The reminder is set and shown entirely on your phone. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn it off at any time in the game's settings.

Internet use

The game plays offline, except for live rooms and the leaderboard. It connects to Google Play, on your phone, to check what has been bought, to handle purchases, to download the Forest Realm and game updates, and to show its rating card; to our server while you are in a live room; to our server for news, offers and update notices; to send anonymous gameplay stats, unless you turned them off; and, unless you removed yourself from the leaderboard, to send your scores and to show the boards. Offline, the leaderboard says so, the last news the game received stays, and everything else works the same.

Purchases and promo codes

The full game and the optional Supporter Pack are one-time purchases processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. The game only asks Google Play which of them have been bought on your Google account. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. Because the purchase is tied to your Google account, reinstalling the game or using another phone with the same account restores it. Promo codes are redeemed in the Google Play Store.

Children

PaperShot is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only things a player types are a short display name for 2-player games, which is shown to the other player and deleted when the live room closes, and a leaderboard name, which is shown on the public boards until the player removes it (a generated name is used otherwise).
